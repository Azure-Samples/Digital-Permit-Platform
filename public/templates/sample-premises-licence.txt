ANNEX 1 — MANDATORY CONDITIONS

1. No supply of alcohol may be made under this licence at a time when there is no
   designated premises supervisor in respect of this licence, or at a time when the
   designated premises supervisor does not hold a personal licence or the personal
   licence is suspended.

2. Every supply of alcohol under this licence must be made or authorised by a person
   who holds a personal licence.

3. (1) The responsible person must ensure that staff on relevant premises do not carry
   out, arrange or participate in any irresponsible promotions. An irresponsible
   promotion includes drinking games, unlimited or unspecified quantities of alcohol
   for a fixed or discounted fee, and the dispensing of alcohol directly into the mouth.

4. The responsible person must ensure that free potable water is provided on request
   to customers where it is reasonably available.

5. (1) The premises licence holder or club premises certificate holder must ensure that
   an age verification policy applies to the premises in relation to the sale or supply
   of alcohol. (2) The policy must require individuals who appear to the responsible
   person to be under 18 years of age to produce, on request, identification bearing
   their photograph, date of birth and a holographic mark or ultraviolet feature.

6. The responsible person must ensure that measures are made available on request:
   beer or cider — half pint; gin, rum, vodka or whisky — 25ml or 35ml; still wine
   in a glass — 125ml; and that customers are made aware of them.

7. Alcohol must not be sold below the permitted price (the level of duty plus VAT).

ANNEX 2 — CONDITIONS CONSISTENT WITH THE OPERATING SCHEDULE

General
8.  The premises shall operate a Challenge 25 age verification policy. Any person who
    appears to be under 25 years of age shall be asked to provide photographic proof of
    age before being served alcohol. Acceptable forms of identification are a passport,
    a photocard driving licence, a Ministry of Defence card, or a PASS-accredited card.

9.  A refusals register shall be maintained at the premises and completed each time a
    sale of alcohol is refused. The register shall be made available to an authorised
    officer of the Council or the police on request.

10. All staff involved in the sale of alcohol shall receive training in the licensing
    objectives, the age verification policy and the conditions of this licence before
    their first sale, with refresher training at least every six months. Written records
    of training shall be kept for at least twelve months.

Prevention of Crime and Disorder (following representations by Contoso Police)
11. A digital CCTV system shall be installed and maintained to a standard agreed with
    Contoso Police. The system shall cover all entrances and exits and all areas where
    alcohol is sold or consumed, shall operate at all times the premises are open, and
    recordings shall be retained for a minimum of 28 days and supplied to the police on
    request.

12. From 21:00 on Fridays and Saturdays, and on any day when the premises are open after
    00:00, a minimum of two Security Industry Authority (SIA) licensed door supervisors
    shall be on duty. A register of door supervisors on duty shall be maintained.

13. The premises shall be a member of the Contoso Pubwatch scheme and shall operate the
    town centre radio link when open after 23:00.

Prevention of Public Nuisance
14. Prominent, clear notices shall be displayed at all exits requesting customers to
    respect local residents and leave the area quietly.

15. After 23:00 the outside area to the front of the premises shall not be used by more
    than 10 customers at any one time, and shall not be used at all after 00:30. All
    doors and windows shall be kept closed after 23:00 when regulated entertainment is
    taking place, except for access and egress.

16. A written dispersal policy shall be in place and all staff made aware of it.
